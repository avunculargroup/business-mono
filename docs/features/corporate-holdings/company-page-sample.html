What this means for an Australian CFO. Under the revaluation model a rising bitcoin price produces no reported profit. Under the US model it produces reported profit directly. Commentary about bitcoin treasury companies marking to market describes a regime that does not apply here, even when the company describing it is quoted on the ASX.

Locate's own disclosure names the consequence: the treatment may produce periods where statutory earnings do not reflect the long-term strategy, and significant downward revaluations could create compliance pressure against debt covenants.

Mandate

  • Permitted assets — bitcoin only. No other digital asset class. PDS · regulated_disclosure
  • Liquidity gate — cash reserves must at all times exceed the forecast cash required to meet covenant requirements by more than 20%, per management's most recent monthly cashflow forecast.
  • Authority — transactions above a prescribed limit require prior Board approval; below it, senior executives may authorise provided liquidity buffers remain intact.
  • Dual control — all transactions authorised by at least two directors or senior executives. Settlement funds released only under dual authorisation.
  • Disposal — not anticipated other than to maintain liquidity buffers or comply with covenants.

The most transferable artefact on this page. An unlisted Australian company can lift the structure without lifting the thesis.

Custody

Held with Zodia, an institutional custodian whose shareholders include Standard Chartered Bank, SBI Group, Northern Trust and National Australia Bank. Transferred to the custodian immediately on settlement and not retained with brokers. Segregated accounts. Uncapped liability against the custodian for fraud or wilful misconduct. PDS pp.12–13 · regulated_disclosure

No insurance in place. The company was engaging brokers about bitcoin insurance but had arranged none as at the offer date.

Intends independent quarterly reviews reconciling custodian statements against blockchain records, and consideration of SOC 2 Type II or ISAE 3402 reports where custodians make them available.

Source conflict, resolved against the regulated document. The company's About page states it self-custodies with no counterparty risk and no reliance on intermediaries. The offer document names a third-party custodian and lists custodian insolvency as a key risk. Capital mechanism

Accumulation, Jan – Dec 2025

Funded entirely by newly issued equity — a wholesale placement, an at-the-market facility with the broker retaining control of timing, volume and minimum price, and the NZX offer. Not from operating cash flow.

Posture change, Jan – Jul 2026

The issuance facility sat undrawn for a full quarter while an on-market buyback ran, with a royalty obligation settled in scrip. The accumulation flywheel and the buyback point in opposite directions on the share register.

Stated as mechanism. Any reading of what it implies about management's view of the share price is restricted.

Debt

A$4,000,000 outstanding to Pure Asset Management at 30 June 2025, secured over all present and after-acquired property of the group. EBITDA covenant hurdles missed for four disclosed quarters; all waived. The offer document assesses the likelihood of further breaches as moderate to high.
